# Ember Court

An original turn-based card duel, built for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-card-game-20261122-1826) ($50, top 10 share).

You and the Ember Queen each have 20 life. Mana grows by 1 every turn (max 10). Play creatures and spells from your hand, attack with your creatures, and reduce the rival to 0 life to win.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html` in a desktop browser. Mouse to play cards and attack, Space ends your turn, 1–7 plays a hand card, Esc returns to the title.
